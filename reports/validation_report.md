# Monthly validation

Each month from December 2025 to May 2026 uses earlier sales only. There are 10,069 homes per task.

Without asking price: median error 7.44%, mean error 9.87%.

With asking price: median error 4.02%, mean error 5.09%.

These months were viewed in earlier work. They are not an untouched final test. See report/report.pdf for model selection, the four month horizon check and limitations.
